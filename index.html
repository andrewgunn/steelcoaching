      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      I've never done a Hyrox. Am I fit enough to start?

      Yes. Most of our members start exactly where you are. We benchmark you on day one and every workout is scaled, so you'll train at the right level and build up to the full race.

      What actually is Hyrox?

      A fitness race: 8 × 1 km runs, each followed by a functional station: SkiErg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, row, farmers carry, sandbag lunges and wall balls. Everyone does the same course, so you can compare times anywhere in the world. Explore the course →

      Should I race Open, Pro or Doubles?

      For a first race, Open or Doubles is the usual start. Doubles splits the station work with a partner, which makes it a great way in. Pro is for experienced racers who want the heavier weights. We'll help you choose on your free call.

      How long do I need to train for my first race?

      If you're already active, 12 weeks of focused training is a good block. Starting from scratch, give yourself 16–20 weeks. Use the race countdown to see what phase you're in.

      How many sessions a week should I do?

      We recommend 3 sessions a week to start, mixing station work, running and strength. Recovery matters, so we'll build up to more as your body adapts.

      Do you help with nutrition and recovery?

      Yes. We give simple, practical guidance on eating, sleep and recovery, plus race-day fuelling for your Hyrox.

      What happens in a session?

      Each class is 60 minutes and led by a qualified coach: a warm-up, a briefing on the movements and form, the workout with coaching throughout, then a cool-down together.

      What makes Steel different from other gyms near me?

      We're locally owned and purpose-built to make an impact on health and happiness in Sheffield. You're not a number here. Everyone knows your name, and there's no judgement, ever.
